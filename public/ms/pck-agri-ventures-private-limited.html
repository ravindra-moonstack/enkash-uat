About Us

PCK Agri Ventures Private Limited, established in 2020, is a leading manufacturer, exporter, and supplier of premium spices and agro-commodities. Backed by over six decades of expertise from the PCK Group, we operate a state-of-the-art spice processing and packing unit in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, and additional facilities in Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, and Rajasthan. Our products, marketed under the brand name MAAVAN, include high-quality spices like coriander, cumin, fennel, and spice powders, as well as soya-based frozen products. With a commitment to quality, hygiene, and customer satisfaction, we cater to both domestic and international markets, earning recognition as one of India’s top spice exporters.
