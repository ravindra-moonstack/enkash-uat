About Us

Super Gas Co, based in Karnataka, India, is a leading distributor of gaseous fuels, committed to providing safe, reliable, and eco-friendly energy solutions. Established with a vision to power homes and industries, we specialize in the supply of LPG and other gaseous fuels, ensuring quality and safety at every step. Our advanced infrastructure and dedicated team enable us to serve a diverse range of customers, from households to commercial enterprises, across Karnataka and beyond.
