About Us

Established in 1998, Maruthi Agencies has emerged as a well-recognized name in the footwear industry in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. As a leading manufacturer, trader, and wholesaler, we specialize in offering a diverse range of high-quality footwear, including gents sandals, men's sports shoes, gents slippers, ladies slippers, and ladies canvas shoes. Under the leadership of Mr. H. Jitendar, we are committed to delivering exceptional products that meet the needs of our valued clients. Our dedication to quality, customer satisfaction, and competitive pricing has earned us a strong reputation and a loyal clientele across the region.
