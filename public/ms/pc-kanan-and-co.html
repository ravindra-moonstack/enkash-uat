About Us

P.C. Kannan & Co, established in 2017 and registered in Gujarat, India, is a trusted name in the wholesale and retail distribution of pharmaceutical goods. As a partnership firm, we are committed to ensuring the timely supply of high-quality medicines and healthcare products to pharmacies, hospitals, and healthcare providers. Our dedication to reliability, quality, and customer satisfaction has made us a preferred partner in the pharmaceutical supply chain across Gujarat and beyond.
