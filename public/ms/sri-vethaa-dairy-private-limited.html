About Us

Sri Vethaa Dairy Private Limited, established in 1996 in Musiri, Tamil Nadu, is dedicated to delivering high-quality, farm-fresh milk and dairy products. With a legacy spanning over two decades, we prioritize health and nutrition, ensuring our products retain their natural taste and flavor. Our state-of-the-art facilities and automated processes guarantee safe, wholesome dairy products that meet stringent quality standards. We are proud to be a trusted household name, committed to bringing the finest dairy foods to families across Tamil Nadu.
